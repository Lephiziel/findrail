# Community conduct

Treat contributors and users with respect. Discuss ideas and code without
personal attacks. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, and deliberate
publication of private information are unacceptable.

Give useful technical feedback, disclose conflicts of interest, and respect
maintainers' and contributors' time. Do not use issues for spam or unsolicited
promotion.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and restrict participation when
needed. For a sensitive concern, request a private contact channel without
posting identifying or confidential details publicly.
