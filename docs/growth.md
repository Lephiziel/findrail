# Adoption and sustainable development

## Adoption hypothesis

People adopt Findrail because it finds information across sources with low setup
effort and inspectable data ownership. Stars are a visibility indicator; recurring
use and unsolicited contributions are stronger signals of product value.

## Positioning to validate

Onyx and Khoj already cover connected knowledge and personal AI. Findrail should
test whether a smaller local install, useful keyword search without a required
model account, source-backed previews, and transparent freshness are enough to
win a specific audience. Do not claim those features are absent from competitors
without a versioned comparison.

References: [Onyx](https://github.com/onyx-dot-app/onyx),
[Khoj](https://github.com/khoj-ai/khoj).

## First public demonstration

Use an entirely public demo corpus. Search a remembered phrase across a local
note and a GitHub issue, inspect the evidence, update the source, and show the
fresh result. Until GitHub is implemented, demonstrations must show local
sources only and label future product mockups accurately.

## Launch sequence

1. Publish a coherent repository with a runnable demo and honest status.
2. Recruit independent testers who have real information-finding tasks.
3. Fix install and search-quality failures before widening connector coverage.
4. Publish reproducible comparisons, resource measurements, and short demos.
5. Submit relevant community posts and catalog entries when their rules permit.
6. Offer well-scoped issues and a connector conformance harness for contributors.

No purchased stars, bulk unsolicited outreach, invented endorsements, or claims
that a roadmap capability is already supported.

## Metrics

Time to first useful result, installation completion, weekly returning testers,
task success, missed-result reports, query latency on declared corpora, sync
recovery, and independent contributor completion. Keep measurement voluntary
and aggregate; the current runtime has no telemetry collection.

## Sustainable funding options

Possible routes are sponsorship, paid support, and operated connector-sync or
encrypted-storage infrastructure after demand is demonstrated. These are
business hypotheses, not revenue predictions. The Apache-2.0 local product
remains independently usable; hosting revenue should pay for operations rather
than remove basic local capabilities.
